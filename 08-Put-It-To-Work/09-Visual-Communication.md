# 🧠 Core Communication Strategies

There are three primary communication tools in cybersecurity:

    📊 Visual Dashboards
    📧 Structured Emails
    📞 Direct Communication (Call / IM)

Choosing the right one is a professional skill.

---

# 📊 1. Visual Dashboards (Best for Data & Metrics)

A visual dashboard displays:

    Charts
    Graphs
    Tables
    Infographics

Visuals help stakeholders:

- Quickly understand trends
- Compare departments
- Spot risk patterns
- Make faster decisions

Busy executives prefer visuals.

---

# 🛑 When to Use Visuals

Use dashboards when:

✔ Communicating numbers  
✔ Comparing departments  
✔ Showing trends over time  
✔ Presenting audit findings  
✔ Reporting phishing click rates  
✔ Showing incident frequency  

Avoid visuals when:

❌ Sensitive narrative explanation is required  
❌ Confidential details shouldn’t be widely shared  
❌ The issue is urgent and immediate  

---

# 📈 Example: Phishing Click Report

Scenario:

CISO wants to know which departments click phishing emails most often.

Data:

    Human Resources – 30
    Customer Service – 18
    Global Security – 10
    Media Relations – 40
    Professional Development – 27

Best method:

    Bar Chart Visualization

Why?

Because numbers are easier to understand visually than in paragraph form.

# 📧 2. Email Communication

Best for:

✔ Structured updates  
✔ Detailed explanations  
✔ Moderate urgency incidents  
✔ Documentation trail  

---

## Email Best Practices

Be:

    Clear
    Concise
    Purpose-driven

Include:

1. What happened  
2. Why it matters  
3. Recommended action  
4. Required timeline  

---

## ⚠ Sensitive Email Warning

Before sending:

✔ Verify recipient  
✔ Follow playbook procedures  
✔ Confirm confidentiality level  

Sending security information to the wrong person  
=  
New security incident.

---

# 📞 3. Direct Communication (Phone / Instant Message)

Best for:

✔ Urgent incidents  
✔ Time-sensitive escalation  
✔ Follow-ups on unanswered emails  

If email response is delayed:

    Send a polite follow-up
    Or make a short call

Pro tip:

    Direct communication prevents small issues from becoming major incidents.

---

# 🧩 Choosing the Right Method

Simple issue:

    Instant message

Moderate issue:

    Structured email

Complex incident:

    Detailed report + meeting

Data-heavy situation:

    Visual dashboard

Urgent situation:

    Phone call

# 📖 Case Study: Communication Strategy

---

## 🛑 Incident #1 – Locked Accounts

Situation:
Multiple employees locked out due to failed logins.

Stakeholder:
Senior executives

Best method:
Visual dashboard

Why?

Executives want quick insight into:

- Which departments are affected
- Scale of problem
- Trend patterns

Juliana created charts.

Result:
Clear understanding → Faster decision-making.

---

## 🚨 Incident #2 – Attempted Data Compromise

Situation:
Attacker almost compromised customer data system.

Stakeholder:
CISO

Best method:
Detailed report

Included:

    Timeline
    What worked
    What failed
    Process improvement suggestions

Why?

CISO cares about:

- Controls
- Risk gaps
- Strategic improvements

Visuals alone were not enough.
