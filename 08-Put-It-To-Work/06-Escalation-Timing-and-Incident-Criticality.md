# ⏳ Escalation Timing & Incident Criticality  

---

# 🚨 Why Escalation Timing Matters

A small incident today  
=  
A major breach next week  

Security is not just about detection.  
It is about **timely escalation**.

Delayed escalation can cause:

- Financial loss  
- Operational downtime  
- Data breaches  
- Regulatory penalties  
- Reputation damage  

---

# 📖 Real-World Scenario

You notice unusual log activity in a banned application.

You plan to report it later.

You forget.

One week later:

- Manufacturing site breached  
- Operations halted  
- Company losing money  
- Investigation traces root cause → banned app activity  

Lesson:

Small signals ignored → Large incidents created.

---

# 📊 Incident Criticality Levels

When you first detect an issue, you may not know full impact.

Start with:

    Low
    Medium
    High
    Critical

Example:

✔ Medium → Limited information  
✔ High → Confirmed impact on critical system  
✔ Low → Minimal business impact  

Criticality can change after review by senior incident handler.

---

# 🧠 Asset-Based Prioritization

Not all incidents are equal.

Urgency depends on **affected asset**.

---

## 🟢 Low-Level Asset Example

Employee forgets password  
Multiple failed login attempts  

Impact: Minimal  
Priority: Low  

---

## 🔴 High-Level Asset Example

Unauthorized access to:

- Manufacturing application  
- Customer PII database  
- Financial systems  

Impact: Severe  
Priority: High or Critical  

---

# 🏭 Asset Impact Rule

If incident affects:

- Core operations
- Revenue systems
- Sensitive data
- Legal compliance

Escalate immediately.

---

# 📜 Escalation Policy

Every organization has its own escalation policy.

An escalation policy defines:

- Who gets notified
- When they get notified
- How incidents are escalated
- Backup contacts if supervisor unavailable

There is no universal standard.

You must follow your organization’s policy.

---

# 🧭 General Escalation Guidelines

1. Identify incident type  
2. Identify affected asset  
3. Determine potential impact  
4. Check escalation policy  
5. Escalate to correct person  

If supervisor unavailable → escalate to next designated contact.

---

# 🔍 Attention to Detail

Small details matter:

- Wrong escalation contact = delay  
- Misclassified severity = underreaction  
- Missed log entry = undetected breach  

Security is precision work.
